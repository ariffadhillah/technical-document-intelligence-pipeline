# Maintenance, Diagnostic, and Safety Intelligence

Extract explicitly supported:

- inspection tasks
- maintenance tasks
- repair procedures
- installation tasks
- modification tasks
- restoration tasks
- required tools
- required parts
- measurements
- warnings
- prerequisites
- limitations
- unresolved issues

For diagnostic content, distinguish:

- observed symptom
- possible cause
- confirmed cause
- recommended action
- unresolved diagnosis

Do not transform a speculative forum suggestion into a confirmed diagnostic
finding.

Separate:

- safety warnings
- operational warnings
- legal or registration warnings
- compatibility warnings
- modification risks
- maintenance cautions

Preserve conflicting opinions as separate recommendations or warnings when
the source does not resolve the conflict.
