# Technical Reference Intelligence

Populate `technical_references` with explicitly mentioned:

- manufacturer documents
- service manuals
- workshop manuals
- parts catalogues
- product catalogues
- technical drawings
- schematics
- product datasheets
- brochures
- quotations
- books
- standards
- regulations
- websites
- linked technical pages
- forum threads
- model references
- part references
- document identifiers

For every reference, preserve when available:

- reference type
- exact title
- identifier
- issuing organization
- URL
- description
- evidence

A brochure or quotation embedded in an attachment should be recorded as a
technical reference when its identity is visible.

Do not convert an organization name alone into a technical reference.

Do not invent a title for a document whose title is unavailable.

A vague mention such as "the book" may be recorded only with low confidence,
and no missing title or identifier may be invented.
